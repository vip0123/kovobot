  • No surprises from strangers. Once a Task reads web pages or emails, Pre-approvals are set aside and it asks you again.

Questions

Which models can my Bot use?

Models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI, and many more through OpenRouter. To operate a Computer, a Bot uses an OpenAI, Anthropic or Google model.

Do I need my own API keys?

No. AI use runs on our keys and is paid from Credit. You can also add your own key to a Bot, and calls on it skip Credit and the Fee.

Why do I connect my own Telegram, Discord or Slack app?

Your Bot talks through an app you register and own, so it carries your name and stays under your control. We walk you through setup on our website.

What happens when my Credit runs out?

Your Bot says so and stops. Linux and Windows Computers go Asleep, and we keep their disks for 30 days while we email you. Auto top-up can buy more Credit for you, but only if you switch it on.

Where is my data kept?

In the US. When you ask us to delete your data, we erase it within 30 days. Business Customers can sign a GDPR data processing agreement.

Can I get a refund?

Unspent Top-up credit is refundable within 14 days of purchase. Subscriptions cancel any time and end when the paid month ends.
